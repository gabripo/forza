<p align="center"><b>Help the people you love get stronger and healthier, one small step at a time.</b></p>

---

Our parents are getting older. We know that **strength training, enough protein, walking and good sleep**
keep them independent and healthy for many more years, but "go to the gym and eat better" is not advice
anyone can follow.

**Forza!** is a simple, friendly app that you set up *for* a parent or a relative. It guides them through
beginner-friendly workouts, explains their progress in plain words, estimates meals from a photo or a
barcode, and teaches the basics with printable infographics. You, the family "coach", follow their progress
and adjust their plan from your own computer.

- 🍽️ **"What should I order?"** Photograph a restaurant menu, or say the dishes: a quick AI reads it, a stronger
  one picks the best dish for *your* day so far and says why in everyday words, with one small tweak to ask for.
- 📷 **Snap, say or scan a meal**, and see first what to do ("add some protein"), judged together with the rest
  of that meal and of the day. A meal that looks logged by mistake ("Taralli" saved as 3 g of breadsticks) is
  asked about until it is corrected or confirmed, and any meal can be changed later: foods and amounts. A scanned
  product that is wrong is put right with "Not this one?", for the whole family's next scans.
- 🧑‍🍳 **No idea what to eat? Build your own recipe.** Type, say, scan or photograph what you have at home: up to
  three recipes for your next meal, with amounts that fit what is left of your day.
- 🍦 **Fewer processed foods, kindly.** Whoever often logs ice cream, biscuits or snacks is asked whether they know
  these fill you up less and bring fewer nutrients, with many alternatives just as pleasant, one tap away.
- 📖 **Family recipes, Thermomix included.** Write a dish once (type the ingredients, scan their barcodes or
  photograph them, or the recipe written down, or type their values; or make it from a meal you logged, Build With Science's too), with
  its method step by step (baking brings its temperature, oven mode and time), then anyone logs a portion in one tap. Got a
  Thermomix (Bimby)? Find the recipe in the app or paste a Cookidoo link: the ingredients are read for you, and a
  variant of your own (less butter, another vegetable) is one tap away.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Cook once, log for everyone.** Share a meal with whoever ate it, each with their own percentage of it; the family's
  food database grows with every meal anyone logs. A family coach, chosen in Forza! Coach, can log meals for the
  others too, straight into their food log, and sees the whole family's day on their phone: targets and meals.
- 🧭 **The day and the week at a glance.** A kind score for the whole day, a ring of what each meal brings to the
  day's target (calories, protein, carbs or fat), each meal's breakdown, one sentence of encouragement on Home, and
  every week "What happened last week". After days short of protein (or of calories, when not losing fat), a few
  foods that bring it back, one tap away.
- 🔥 **A target that learns from you.** The energy your body uses is measured more and more from your own food
  and scale, fully after about 4 weeks of logs and weigh-ins (two lessons explain why, and why two people of
  the same weight can need 750 kcal apart); days not all logged are left out (you are asked, and can change it later), intermittent fasting
  is recognised, the formulas are shown and the history is kept.
- 💪 **Workouts that progress by themselves**, and any exercise found from a description ("I raise a knee and
  touch it with the opposite elbow"), with its photos.
- 📈 **Progress for any period**, every chart read with a touch, and the targets over time told as a path.
- 🧘 **Serene, not obsessed.** "Why one day's numbers matter little": consistency and a helpful home beat
  perfect days.

Steps and weight arrive by themselves from **Health Connect** (Android, every hour) or **Apple Health** (iPhone),
so smart scales like **Eufy** just work. Coming from **Build With Science**? The whole workout history comes
along, and it **stays in sync**: meals through Health Connect, new workouts read by the phone itself (or by Forza! Coach).
A friendly, opt-in **family challenge** gives points for showing up (workouts, lessons, steps, meals logged),
never for weight, so a beginner can beat the family athlete.

## Get started

You need a computer and about 30 minutes. No technical skills, no cost.

1. **Install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/en/download)** (the LTS version) and **download Forza!**
   (green **Code** button above → **Download ZIP**, then unzip it).
2. **Double-click "Forza Coach"** in the folder (`Forza Coach (Mac).command`, `Forza Coach (Windows).bat`,
   or `forza-coach-linux.sh`). Follow the four steps on screen: a free Supabase account, your database, an
   optional free key for food photos, install.
3. **Add a person** and let them **scan the QR code** with their phone. Done.

👉 The **[Coach guide](docs/coach-guide.md)** ([italiano](docs/coach-guide.it.md)) walks you through every
step, including health sync, smart scales and the Build With Science import.

## Private and free

- Each family has **its own database** (a free Supabase project that belongs to you). Each person sees only
  their own data. Only the coach can create accounts.
- The master key stays **on the coach's computer**, in its password store (Keychain, Credential Manager or
  the Linux keyring).
- Everything runs on free plans: Supabase, GitHub Pages, Google Gemini (food photos), Open Food Facts.
